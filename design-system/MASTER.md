Design system: Helix Support

- Query: customer support operations console dense utilitarian teal responsive
- Matched profile: saas
- Style direction: minimal-modern
- Palette: neutral paper/surface layers with teal actions, blue focus, amber risk, and red failure states
- Typography: Aptos + Microsoft YaHei + PingFang SC for dense bilingual operations content
- Effects: subtle shadows and crisp borders
- Layout bias: compact three-pane operations workspace with stable controls and no marketing surfaces
- Avoid: low-contrast cards; decorative gradients that hide hierarchy
- Notes: Optimize for clarity and scanability; preserve visible focus rings, crisp borders, stable row geometry, and 390 px mobile drawer behavior.
